About me

Who I am.
What I explore.

My academic and research interests lie at the intersection of Physics, Computational Science, Artificial Intelligence, and emerging technologies.

As a Physics graduate, I am particularly interested in Computational Physics, Machine Learning, Artificial Intelligence, Quantum Mechanics, Theoretical Physics, Classical Mechanics, High Energy Physics, Semiconductor Physics, and Quantum Technologies.

AI × PHYSICS

Understanding intelligent systems.

Recently, I have become increasingly interested in how AI models are trained, the physics and mathematical principles underlying their development, and the ways these systems are reshaping human behaviour, social communication, and algorithmic decision-making. I am especially curious about how algorithms influence, guide, and sometimes constrain human choices, creating new patterns of interaction between people, technology, and society.

COMPUTATION

Modelling the complex.

I am interested in using computational methods and AI to model physical systems, analyse complex datasets, and explore problems at the frontier of scientific discovery and technology. At the same time, I enjoy examining the broader social and philosophical implications of these technologies, particularly their effects on communication, public discourse, individual agency, and collective behaviour.

Beyond the laboratory

Politics. Economics.
Spirituality. Society.

Beyond physics and technology, I enjoy exploring politics, economics, spirituality, and social issues. I am especially drawn to interdisciplinary discussions that connect science and technology with society, culture, and public policy.

AGARTALA · TRIPURA · INDIA

Where I come from.

I come from Agartala, Tripura, in India's beautiful northeastern region. Over the years, I have travelled across much of the country, encountering diverse landscapes, communities, perspectives, and stories. These experiences have shaped the way I understand people, society, and the many different ways in which individuals make sense of the world.
